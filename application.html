Application Techniques & Layering Order

Applying products in the correct sequence ensures active ingredients penetrate effectively without causing irritation.

The Golden Rule: Thinnest to Thickest

Step 1: Cleansing

Massage cleanser onto damp skin using circular motions for 60 seconds. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water to avoid irritating blood vessels.

Step 2: Liquid Serums & Toners

Apply water-based liquids onto slightly damp skin. Gently press formulas into your face and neck using palm pressure rather than harsh rubbing.

Step 3: Treatment Serums

Dispense 3-4 drops of active treatments (such as Vitamin C, Niacinamide, or Retinol). Allow 1-2 minutes for absorption before proceeding.

Step 4: Moisturizers

Smooth creams and emulsions over the entire face to seal in water hydrates and strengthen your lipid barrier.

Step 5: Sunscreen (AM Only)

Apply a nickel-sized amount of sunscreen as your final step every morning. Reapply every two hours when outdoors.

Essential Application Tips

Wait Between Actives

Give strong active ingredients like Vitamin C and exfoliating acids 60 to 90 seconds to absorb before applying subsequent products to preserve formula efficacy.

Apply to Damp Skin

Humectants like Hyaluronic Acid draw moisture into the skin. Apply them onto slightly damp skin after washing to lock in maximum water hydration.

Never Skip the Neck

Extend all serum, moisturizing, and sunscreen applications down your neck and chest. These areas have thin skin that shows early signs of aging.

Patch Test New Products

Before applying a new product to your entire face, dab a small amount behind your ear or on your inner wrist for 24 hours to monitor for allergic reactions.
