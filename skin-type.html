Understanding Your Skin Type

Identifying your skin type is the crucial first step in building a safe and effective daily routine.

The Bare-Face Diagnostic Test

Follow these steps at home to accurately observe how your skin behaves naturally without any product interference:

  1. Wash your face thoroughly with a mild, gentle cleanser to remove oil, makeup, and dirt.
  2. Gently pat your skin dry with a clean towel. Do not apply any serums, moisturizers, or treatments.
  3. Wait exactly 30 minutes in a climate-controlled room. Avoid heavy physical activity during this window.
  4. Examine your skin closely in a well-lit mirror and match your symptoms to the profiles below.

Oily Skin Excess Sebum

Your skin displays a visible shine across the forehead, nose, cheeks, and chin. Pores may appear enlarged or prone to congestion and acne breakouts throughout the day.

Dry Skin Lack of Moisture

Your face feels tight, uncomfortable, or itchy after cleansing. You may notice subtle flaking, rough texture, or fine lines due to reduced natural oil production.

Combination Skin Dual Character

Your T-zone (forehead, nose, and chin) shows shine and oiliness, while your cheeks remain normal or dry. This type requires balanced care for different facial zones.

Sensitive Skin Reactive

Your skin flushes red easily and frequently experiences burning, stinging, or irritation when exposed to new products, weather changes, or active ingredients.

Normal Skin Balanced

Your skin feels comfortable, hydated, and balanced—neither overly shiny nor uncomfortably tight. Breakouts and irritation are rare occurrences.
